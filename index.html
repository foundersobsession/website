Field notes — click to expand

A few raw lessons, while you're here.

Bootstrapped founders often spend more time per rupee than funded ones — every hire, tool, and ad spend has to earn its place immediately. It's not about spending less. It's about spending only on what's already proven.

Not a morning routine. Not a productivity hack. It's answering the customer directly, personally, for far longer than feels efficient — because in the early days, that conversation is the only real market research you can afford.

Zoho took over a decade of quiet, unfunded building before most people had heard of it. The public story usually starts the moment it became impressive to talk about — not the moment it actually started.

Usually just one thing: whether the underlying problem being solved stayed the same. The product can change completely. The moment the "who" and "why" change too, it's a new company wearing the old one's name.
